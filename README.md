## 🧭 Program Management Lifecycle

### 1. 🧩 Intake & Strategy Alignment

**Goal:** Ensure alignment with organizational priorities  

**Includes:**
- Business case evaluation  
- ROI & impact analysis  
- Stakeholder identification  
- Scope definition  
- Prioritization models  

**Outputs:**
- Program Charter  
- High-level roadmap  
- Stakeholder matrix  

---

### 2. 🏗️ Planning & Structuring

**Goal:** Establish a strong execution foundation  

**Includes:**
- Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)  
- Timeline & milestones  
- Resource planning  
- Budget allocation  
- Risk identification  

**Outputs:**
- Execution roadmap  
- RAID log  
- Resource plan  

---

### 3. ⚙️ Execution & Delivery

**Goal:** Deliver efficiently with quality  

**Includes:**
- Sprint / milestone tracking  
- Cross-team coordination  
- Delivery monitoring  
- Issue resolution  
- Change management  

**Outputs:**
- Status reports  
- Delivery dashboards  
- Change logs  

---

### 4. 📊 Monitoring & Performance

**Goal:** Ensure visibility and control  

**Key Metrics:**
- Schedule variance  
- Budget variance  
- Resource utilization  
- Delivery velocity  
- Defect leakage  

**Outputs:**
- Program health reports (RAG)  
- KPI dashboards  
- Executive summaries  

---

### 5. ⚠️ Risk & Escalation Management

**Goal:** Proactively mitigate risks  

**Includes:**
- Risk tracking  
- Issue prioritization  
- Escalation handling  
- Mitigation planning  

**Outputs:**
- RAID updates  
- Escalation reports  
- Mitigation plans  

---

### 6. 🏁 Closure & Value Realization

**Goal:** Ensure business outcomes are achieved  

**Includes:**
- Delivery validation  
- KPI vs actual comparison  
- Stakeholder sign-off  
- Retrospectives  
- Knowledge transfer  

**Outputs:**
- Closure report  
- Lessons learned  
- Value realization report
