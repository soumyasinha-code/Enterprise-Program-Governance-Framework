# 📦 Case Study 1: Pixelbin — Scalable Digital Asset Management & CDN Optimization

> Delivering a high-performance Digital Asset Management (DAM) platform with real-time media transformation and CDN-based delivery at scale.

---

## 🚀 Overview

Pixelbin is a **Digital Asset Management (DAM) platform** that enables organizations to store, transform, optimize, and deliver media assets (images & videos) efficiently using CDN infrastructure.

This case study outlines how a **scalable, enterprise-grade media delivery system** was governed, executed, and optimized across multiple stakeholders and high-volume environments.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Enterprises managing large-scale digital commerce platforms faced:

- Slow image/video load times impacting user experience  
- High media storage and delivery costs  
- Lack of real-time media optimization (resizing, compression, format conversion)  
- Inconsistent asset delivery across platforms (Web, Mobile, Apps)  
- Limited visibility into asset performance  

---

## 💡 Solution

Implemented **Pixelbin DAM + CDN-based media optimization pipeline** to enable:

- Real-time media transformations (resize, compress, format conversion)  
- CDN-based low-latency delivery  
- Centralized asset management  
- API-driven integration across platforms
